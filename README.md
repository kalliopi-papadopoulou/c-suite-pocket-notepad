## Executive Overview

**The C-Suite Pocket Notepad** is an institutional-grade quantitative decision-support system and interactive Business Intelligence (BI) HUD designed for luxury hospitality executives, asset managers, private equity stakeholders, and hospitality technology architects.

It translates **36 microeconomic and hospitality management frameworks**, live operational telemetry, **USALI (Uniform System of Accounts for the Lodging Industry)** fundamentals, and **4-Statement Financial Accounting** into immediate margin expansion and capital allocation clarity across **USD ($)**, **EUR (€)**, and **CHF (Fr.)**.

## Key Capabilities & 7 Interactive BI Modules

The system synthesizes 36 quantitative formulas and institutional accounting standards into **Seven Interactive Executive Dashboards**:

### 1. Asset, CAPEX, OPEX & Break-Even CVP (`#1, #2, #7`)
* **Capital Expenditure (CAPEX) Portfolio Split:** Balances Maintenance CAPEX (4%–5% FF&E reserve), Growth CAPEX (renovations, footprint expansion), and Strategic Technology CAPEX (cloud PMS/POS, smart HVAC, solar microgrids).
* **Operating Expense Ratio (OER) & Break-Even Point (BEP):** Computes exact monthly break-even room volume, occupancy percentage, and calendar day of break-even achievement.
* **BI HUD Visualizers:** Interactive Cost-Volume-Profit (CVP) Crosshair Line Chart and CAPEX Allocation Doughnut Chart.

### 2. Labor Productivity, Capacity Utilization & Queue Physics (`#3, #6a, #6b`)
* **Departmental Labor Economics:** Tracks Total Payroll %, Housekeeping Minutes Per Occupied Room (MPR), and F&B Productivity Index (Covers per Labor Hour).
* **Little's Law Queue Physics ($L = \lambda \times W$):** Models lobby arrival surges ($\lambda$) and processing duration ($W$). Demonstrates the mathematical impact of maintaining a **traditional Front Desk for standard guests** while routing target VIP/group cohorts through an **auxiliary Smart Travel Hub** ($W = 2.0\text{ mins}$), compressing lobby queue load ($L$) by 67%.
* **BI HUD Visualizers:** Non-Linear Queue Surge Curve and Departmental Efficiency vs. Luxury Benchmark Grouped Bar Chart.

### 3. Supply Chain, Par Stock & EOQ U-Curve Mechanics (`#4, #5, #8`)
* **Working Capital Velocity:** Calculates Inventory Turnover, Days Sales of Inventory (DSI), and automated Par Stock Reorder Triggers ($\text{Lead-Time Demand} + \text{Safety Stock}$).
* **Economic Order Quantity ($\text{EOQ} = \sqrt{\frac{2DS}{H}}$):** Pinpoints the exact order batch size that minimizes combined holding and ordering friction.
* **BI HUD Visualizers:** Interactive 3-Line EOQ Cost U-Curve (`Framework #8: Visualizing Inventory Costs`).

### 4. Guest Lifetime Economics, Departmental NPS & Cost of Quality (`#9–#15`)
* **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) & Experience ROI:** Computes Net CLV, CLV-to-CAC multiples, and the **10% Empowered Frontline Comp Ceiling** for instant service recovery.
* **Departmental Net Promoter Score (NPS):** Isolates sentiment symmetry across Front Desk, F&B, and Housekeeping.
* **Cost of Quality (CoQ) & Recovery Savings:** Contrasts Preventive QA investment against Internal/External Failure costs and quantifies total VIP CLV equity preserved.
* **BI HUD Visualizers:** Departmental NPS Symmetry Radar Chart and CoQ vs. Saved Equity Bar Chart.

### 5. USALI P&L Waterfall, Liquidity & DuPont Ratios (`#16–#26`)
* **USALI Flow-Through Diagnostics:** Tracks Gross Revenue, Departmental Expenses, Undistributed Operating Expenses, Gross Operating Profit (GOP), and Net Profit Margin (NPM).
* **Solvency & Institutional Returns:** Computes Current Liquidity Ratio, Net Working Capital, Annualized Return on Assets (ROA), and Return on Equity (ROE).
* **BI HUD Visualizers:** USALI Income Statement Waterfall Bar Chart and Horizontal DuPont Return Matrix.

### 6. Commercial Revenue Management & Yield Command Center (`#27–#36`)
* **Per-Available-Room Hierarchy:** Evaluates Occupancy %, ADR, RevPAR, Total RevPAR (TRevPAR), and Gross Operating Profit Per Available Room (GOPPAR).
* **STR Benchmarking & Pricing Realization:** Computes the RevPAR Generation Index (RGI) against competitive sets and Yield Efficiency % against Potential Average Rack Rate.
* **BI HUD Visualizers:** Multi-Metric STR Per-Available-Room Hierarchy Combo Chart.

### 7. Institutional 4-Statement Financial Engine (`P&L • Balance Sheet • Cash Flow • Equity`)
* **I. Income Statement (USALI P&L):** Full top-to-bottom revenue and expense waterfall with live Vertical Common-Size % on every line item.
* **II. Institutional Balance Sheet:** Enforces real-time accounting equilibrium ($\text{Total Assets} = \text{Total Liabilities} + \text{Shareholders' Equity}$).
* **III. Statement of Cash Flows (Indirect Method):** Reconciles Operating Cash Flow (OCF), Investing Cash Flow (CAPEX), Free Cash Flow ($\text{FCF} = \text{OCF} - \text{CAPEX}$), Financing Cash Flow (CFF), and Ending Cash.
* **IV. Statement of Shareholders' Equity:** Tracks Opening Balance, Net Income additions, Capital Contributions, Dividends Paid, and Closing Book Equity.

## Core Methodology & Strategic Governance

1. **Dual-Track Hospitality Service Architecture:** Preserves high-touch traditional Front Office operations for standard guests while deploying auxiliary Smart Travel Hub infrastructure for targeted VIP and high-velocity cohorts.
2. **Multi-Currency Tri-Conversion Engine:** Every financial input and output is simultaneously evaluated in **USD ($)**, **EUR (€)**, and **CHF (Fr.)** with customizable real-time FX conversion rates for cross-border asset governance (EU/Greece, Switzerland/DACH, North America).
3. **Zero-Footprint Client-Side Execution:** All financial simulations, URL state encodings, and workbook exports execute 100% locally in the browser memory—ensuring zero server-side persistence of proprietary hotel P&L data.

## Shareholder Distribution & Executive Export Suite

Built directly into the telemetry command bar for boardroom and investor communications:
* **Share Link (Encrypted URL State):** Compresses all active inputs, currency settings, and tab selections into a Base64 query parameter (`?scenario=...`) so shareholders can open the exact live model on any device.
* **Visual Card (High-DPI PNG):** Renders a crisp 2x-scale dark-mode boardroom card of the active HUD stamped with the author's executive attribution seal.
* **Boardroom View (16:9 Presentation Toggle):** Collapses input slicers and expands visual telemetry across the full viewport for live investor briefings.
* **Export XLSX (Multi-Tab Excel Workbook):** Generates a 5-sheet `.xlsx` financial model (`Executive_KPIs`, `Income_Statement`, `Balance_Sheet`, `Cash_Flow`, `Shareholders_Equity`).
* **Export CSV (Power BI / Tableau Feed):** Downloads a structured multi-currency dataset ready for direct ingestion into Power BI Desktop or Tableau.
* **Print Brief (Full 7-Module Executive Dossier):** Compiles all 7 modules, calibrated inputs, high-resolution chart snapshots, and all 4 financial statements into a clean, multi-page print/PDF brief (automatically excluding web navigation and footer elements).

## Technology Stack & Deployment

* **Core Architecture:** Semantic HTML5, Vanilla ES6+ JavaScript (Zero-Dependency Reactive Calculation Engine).
* **Styling & Typography:** Tailwind CSS (`navy` & `gold` executive design tokens), Google Fonts (`Cinzel` serif display, `Inter` sans-serif UI, `JetBrains Mono` financial telemetry).
* **Data Visualization & Export Engines:**
  * [Chart.js](https://www.chartjs.org/) — Real-time canvas rendering for CVP lines, EOQ U-curves, NPS radars, and USALI waterfalls.
  * [SheetJS (`xlsx`)](https://sheetjs.com/) — Client-side multi-tab Excel `.xlsx` workbook generation.
  * [html2canvas](https://html2canvas.hertzen.com/) — High-DPI DOM-to-Canvas visual brief rendering.
* **Deployment:** Hosted on **GitHub Pages** via direct branch deployment (`main` / root), editable on mobile or desktop via **Visual Studio Code for the Web (`vscode.dev`)**.
